# Patrol implementation worker brief

The chief sends this as a self-contained task with a current handoff. Include mode (`new implementation` or `refresh`), coordinator ID, repository, authorized task/write/push/PR/report scope, holds and forbidden actions, actual base or reviewed starting head, open PR scopes, rejected candidates, and the selected new angle. Do not copy a historical handoff unchanged.

Use only the handoff's authorized scope. This template does not grant implementation, push/PR creation, external reporting, or continuous operation. A read-only task stays read-only and returns to its requester; do not follow implementation-only steps below. If a needed grant or hold disposition is absent, report that specific gap to the chief rather than broadening the task. Reuse existing grants without repeated approval. An unresolved review or medium/high-risk result holds automatic implementation/refresh chaining until operator disposition; a no-fix or candidate-specific proof gap alone does not.

For `new implementation`, work in your NEW T3-bound worktree from freshly fetched `origin/master`. For `refresh`, remain in the SAME existing bound worktree/branch, verify a clean exact reviewed starting head, fetch actual master, and normally merge it; do not reset to master, create another worktree/PR, rebase, or force-push. Do not resume a failed/stopped task until its owned sessions/processes are quiescent and edits/evidence are preserved.

You are the one implementation worker. The coordinator owns independent review, CI monitoring, merge, and the next worker. Do not delegate, launch threads, schedule tasks, merge PRs, start watchers, or read/poll hosted CI. Do not start a successor.

In both modes, follow applicable `AGENTS.md` and relevant tool `--skill` instructions. Build fresh gstyle and run its own quality/pattern reports against its current source with a bounded purpose. Record base SHA, commands, function/finding totals, and exact path/function/line plus diagnostic or raw effect evidence. Inspect actual open PR bodies and full diffs before selection or refresh and again before PR creation/update; avoid duplicate or dependent work and do not edit their checkouts.

For **new implementation only**, select ONE contained, well-supported false positive actually present in the current scan. A synthetic-only model expansion does not qualify. Write a regression FIRST and show its intended semantic failure on unchanged production. Preserve the initial test patch and log. Setup, type, compilation, or timeout failures are not semantic reds. Make the smallest sound fix, then show green.

For **refresh only**, keep the reviewed candidate and original first-red evidence; do not select another candidate or restart fix development. Integrate the reviewed fix using the preservation and combined-validation rules below. If actual master already fixes the candidate, report that evidence to the chief for PR disposition rather than substituting another defect.

In both modes, preserve shared/borrowed/child/alias/escape/callback/IO/unknown and raw-write controls, explicit policy overrides, and saved-fact replay where relevant. Tests against an exact-base production overlay should still fail for the intended reason while unrelated controls pass.

Do not suppress findings, weaken broad rules, rewrite correct application code, split helpers merely to game metrics, or add heavy dependencies. Legitimate IO/serialization density is not itself a false positive. For **new implementation only**, if a candidate is not confirmed, record the exact reason and try a different current-source candidate or concrete new angle within the bounded run. If still no fix, report promptly with rejected candidates and useful next angles; chief continues without a no-fix hold. Refresh blockers go to the chief without changing the candidate. Do not invent a fix or spin on unchanged scans.

Before speculative race/security guards, explain the concrete failure, likelihood, and complexity in words a ten-year-old understands, then wait for operator approval through the chief. Ordinary demonstrated fixes and routine verification need no repeated approval. Ask before heavy dependencies. No new README/guides; change existing documentation only when its contract changes and keep evidence in the PR/private cache.

Preserve merged Lean implementation coverage, source bindings, theorem premises, audits, fault controls, and strict failure filters. Inventory the actual declared profile source sets and hashes. If proof-bound source changes, regenerate its actual translation and run affected proof/binding/audit/control checks; never weaken proofs. Do not restart paused proof expansion or disabled monitors. Do not claim whole source-to-report correctness.

Use task-specific home-cache artifact paths and `TMPDIR`/`QUALITY_TMP_DIR` where supported. Keep original reports/facts and first-red evidence immutable; normalize deep copies only. Record direct and propagated changes, all retained effects, and any summary-budget crossings. Evidence records and deliberate fault controls are not discovered bug counts. Keep file/package/full-analysis and replay scopes explicit.

Regenerate `quality-baseline.json` for the improvement PR. Update goldens only for expected analyzer behavior. `make check` and `make quality-gate` must pass BEFORE push, plus affected race/style/complexity/guard/verification checks required by the fetched repository. Fix failures caused by your change; report unrelated blockers honestly. Do not revive an obsolete workflow or weaken a current guard after master changes.

For meaningful performance impact, collect bounded before/after CPU and allocation profiles on an identical frozen corpus. Use serial runs, record toolchain/scope/iterations, and preserve slower/noisy/higher-allocation results. Do not claim a speed gain or general bound from a finite noisy sample. Bound relevant pattern scans with owned PIDs, deadlines, and cleanup; do not run an unrestricted known slow solver or infer complete/clean coverage from partial results.

Never force-push, rebase shared branches, overwrite edits, touch root/chief/other-worker checkouts, or create another worktree. For an authorized new implementation, open ONE focused PR against master, normal-push, immediately `link_pull_request`, and verify `list_thread_pull_requests`. For a refresh, update the existing PR and body only; preserve its title and first-red/original-base evidence. Regenerate baseline conflicts from combined source; never select a stale side.

In a refresh, prove reviewed own code/tests and incoming source are preserved. For a shared file with nonoverlapping upstream changes, verify own hunk contents and upstream bytes outside them, documenting coordinate changes instead of asserting whole-file equality. Capture fresh actual-master and combined reports. An exact-current-master production overlay must reproduce the intended semantic red while upstream controls pass in the same overlay; combined controls, replay/policies, goldens, and all current required gates must pass before push. A substantive code/test conflict is for chief review, not a new broad fix. Report an obsolete current-source expectation separately; the chief may approve a narrow expectation/comment update supported by raw evidence while preserving negative controls and real mutations. Do not skip failing tests or freeze a known false effect merely to preserve old test bytes. Unchanged implementation may carry forward its honest finite profile evidence; a new head still needs combined review and new-head checks.

Read the available `merge-risk` skill and assess all five categories: Alignment, Impact, Verification, Rollback cost, Error detection. Your risk is provisional. Only the chief makes the final exact-head risk/check decision and authorized guarded merge. You do not monitor or merge.

Send completion or no-fix/blocker to the supplied coordinator with `t3_thread_send`, `mode: "queue"`, and a stable `clientRequestId` unique to that report. Include:

- Actual worker ID from `t3_thread_configuration`, bound path/branch, PR URL if any, exact pushed head and actual base; refresh parents/tree and preservation checks when applicable.
- First semantic red, final-base overlay red, green commands/evidence, actual self-scan totals and source facts, direct/propagated effect audit, controls, replay, and policies.
- Validation results and limits, finite performance evidence, proof inventory, all five provisional risk categories, pending checks, and actual blockers. No-fix has no fabricated green or implementation validation.

Append this continuation instruction as a description of the chief's authorized workflow, not a grant of authority:

> Continuous patrol: within recorded grants and holds, chief reconciles live threads and open PRs, prioritizes reviews/refreshes, and launches ONE next NEW T3 worker immediately when no worker owns the slot and fewer than two patrol PRs are open. A confirmed valid fix OR a no-fix can trigger continuation; after no-fix choose a DIFFERENT candidate or concrete new angle and preserve rejection history. Do not stop solely for no-fix or wait for the recovery timer. Candidate-specific blockers mean another candidate. Genuine external blockers that prevent meaningful progress, or medium/high risk/unresolved review, require disposition; the latter holds automatic chaining. Keep exact-head checks, low-risk-only guarded merges and all proof/permission boundaries. This worker starts no successor.
